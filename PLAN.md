# HS501 — IPR & Cyber Law — PLAN

**Semester:** 5 · **Credits:** 2 · **Level:** Advanced
**Theory:** 30 hrs · **Case Work:** 15 hrs · **Tools:** Case studies, legal texts, policy documents

## Syllabus

### Unit 1 — Introduction to Intellectual Property (6 lectures)
1. What intellectual property is — and why it matters for software
2. Types of IP — copyright, patents, trademarks, trade secrets, designs
3. IP as an economic asset
4. International frameworks — WIPO, TRIPS, Berne Convention
5. IP in India — the legal landscape
6. Software and IP — the overlaps and gaps

### Unit 2 — Copyright in Software (6 lectures)
1. What copyright protects — expression, not ideas
2. Software copyright — source code, object code, documentation
3. Rights of the author — reproduction, distribution, adaptation
4. Fair use and fair dealing
5. Open-source licenses — MIT, GPL, Apache, AGPL in legal terms
6. Copyright infringement — civil and criminal remedies

### Unit 3 — Patents and Software (6 lectures)
1. What a patent is — novelty, non-obviousness, utility
2. Patentable subject matter — software patents debate
3. Software patents in India, US, and EU
4. Patent filing process — provisional and complete
5. Patent infringement — claims, defences, remedies
6. Alternatives — defensive publications, trade secrets

### Unit 4 — Cyber Law Fundamentals (6 lectures)
1. What cyber law covers
2. The Information Technology Act, 2000 (India) — scope and key sections
3. Cybercrimes — hacking, identity theft, fraud, cyberstalking
4. Data protection — GDPR, India DPDP Act 2023
5. Intermediary liability — Section 79 and safe harbour
6. Jurisdiction and cross-border issues

### Unit 5 — Emerging Issues and Practice (6 lectures)
1. AI and IP — authorship, ownership, infringement
2. NFTs and blockchain — ownership questions
3. Deepfakes and synthetic media — legal responses
4. Digital contracts and electronic signatures
5. Compliance for startups — privacy policy, terms of service
6. Careers in IP and cyber law

## Practical Labs (15)

1. Classify five everyday items by type of IP
2. Read and analyse the MIT license
3. Compare MIT, GPL, and Apache — legal differences
4. Analyse an open-source license violation case
5. Read a software patent — identify claims and scope
6. Patent search on Google Patents for a real technology
7. Draft a provisional patent application (exercise)
8. Read the key sections of the IT Act, 2000
9. Case study — a cybercrime prosecution
10. GDPR vs DPDP — a comparison writeup
11. Read a real privacy policy and critique it
12. Draft a privacy policy for a fictional app
13. Analyse a copyright infringement case
14. AI authorship — write up your position with sources
15. Final memo — advise a fictional startup on IP + privacy compliance
